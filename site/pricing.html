Questions people actually ask

Why is the core free?

Because a manifesto you have to pay to practice isn't a manifesto, it's a paywall. The cores are small, auditable, and dependency-free on purpose. Fleet persistence, registries, and dashboards are roadmap work that will be priced only if and when they become a real service.

What counts as "one system"?

One deployed application with its own seam, ontology, and memory — however many models it calls. A monorepo with three products is three systems; one product with five model providers is one.

Do erasure receipts satisfy GDPR/right-to-be-forgotten?

Receipts are evidence of deletion, not legal advice — your counsel maps them to your obligations. What we provide: predicate erasure, a registry of what was released and when, and exports your auditors can read. On Canopy, deletion SLAs are contractual.

Can I pilot just one organ?

Yes. A design-partner conversation can focus on one boundary—ambiguity, ontology drift, or erasure—without implying that a hosted product already exists.

Not sure which organ you're missing?

Forty-five minutes: you describe the system, we tell you whether it's dying as a hoarder, an amnesiac, or a museum — and what the first month of fixing that looks like.
