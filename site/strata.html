Strata

A model's categories aren't designed — they're sedimented from a particular corpus, written by particular people, at a particular time. Strata makes the sediment visible, versioned, and contestable.

$ cargo run --quiet -p strata-poc -- demo

A shipping manifest for training data

The model does not simply "know" — it remembers what a particular crowd once wrote. A ProvenanceEnvelope binds each ontology snapshot to corpus size, vintage, language shares, labor status, source URIs, and SHA-256 commitments. The comparison result retains both envelopes, so changed meaning cannot travel without its histories.

  • Attach provenance to every versioned ontology artifact you ship
  • Renders as a nutrition-facts block in logs and model cards
  • Committed fixture bytes can be re-verified independently

Compare snapshots without losing their histories

The standalone proof verifies two strict provenance envelopes, probes the same concept, and executes a separately committed case through both deterministic artifacts. Drift becomes concrete, reproducible, and routable: the observed result changes from other / manual-review to chosen / family-support.

  • +chosen, -single_parent, ~nuclear
  • Behavior probe exposes an executed classification and route change
  • Artifact, corpus, and case commitments are enforced before comparison
  • Different artifacts, ontologies, and unknown probes are rejected
  • Exit status 2 routes drift to an accountable owner

The legislated layer over the grown one

Where the sediment is wrong for your users, you overrule it — explicitly. Every rule in the Legislature carries a stated reason, because an override without a reason is just a different unaccountable ontology. And every resolution names which layer answered.

  • Enact and repeal are equally first-class
  • Source::Grown vs Source::Legislated on every answer
  • The audit trail your "AI governance" slide claims you have
governance.rsstrata::Legislature
let mut law = Legislature::new();
law.enact("units", "metric",
          "product ships in the EU");

law.resolve("units", "imperial")
// → metric (legislated: product ships in the EU)

law.resolve("size", "double")
// → double (grown)

Stop pretending the model's output is neutral fact.

Strata is the inspectable, contestable layer between your model's history and your users' present. Grown meaning answers by default; legislated rules overrule it where someone has taken responsibility.
